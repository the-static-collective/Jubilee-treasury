# JUBILEE 002 — TRIBUTARY

Status: proposed design, 2026-11-23. No payment processing is activated by this document. Owner: Jubilee Treasury.

## Thesis
Route contributions through the least expensive eligible rail, not necessarily a separate card transaction per person. Preserve the protocol distinction NEED != CAMPAIGN, PLEDGE != SETTLEMENT, and no Jubilee custody. Do not attempt to evade processor fees or terms through misclassification, self-payments, or unauthorized personal transfers.

## Track B — reusable routing protocol
1. Provider registry: jurisdiction, currency, eligibility, min/max, fixed and percentage charges, FX, on/off-ramp, payout latency, reversibility, privacy, authorization evidence and verified checkout origin. Unknown terms remain unknown. Rank only permitted available routes by estimated net delivery and deadline fit; donor chooses. Never silently redirect to alternate recipients.
2. Station aggregation: independent authorized churches collect through their own permitted channels and settle a batch. Jubilee stores declared aggregate and separately confirmed transfer, not money, balances, transferable credits, or custody. Local collection costs are not assumed zero. A station must not collect on behalf of others without appropriate authority.
3. Sponsor matching: capped institutional commitment, explicit activation criteria and consent, anti-duplicate controls, expiration, auditable allocation, separate fulfillment. An activated match is not money received; no claims of prefunding without proof.
4. Adapters: hosted checkout/payment link first, bank/ACH next (including account verification fees and timing), Lightning invoices via eligible licensed provider or authorized receiver's existing infrastructure. Explore BTCPay, Strike, Interledger/Open Payments in sandbox; account eligibility, network liquidity, conversion spreads and off-ramp fees are mandatory constraints. Crypto is never a shortcut around sanctions, AML, foreign funding or other legal requirements.
5. Evidence ledger: append-only event records with case ID, origin, type, currency, amount in integer minor units, timestamps, source reference, status and idempotency key. Events: PLEDGE, STATION_DECLARED, MATCH_COMMITTED, MATCH_ACTIVATED, PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, REFUND, TRANSFER_CONFIRMED, SETTLEMENT_WITNESSED, WITHDRAWN. Provider webhook signatures and replay checks, event deduplication, refund/chargeback reconciliation, no double counting station constituent gifts and batch transfers. Public view exposes only reviewed aggregate totals with as-of time; personal data private, consent and retention controls. Never publish exact beneficiary location or private financial documents.
6. Boundaries: existing Treasury signed need envelopes remain canonical for portable need; Campfire owns circle-local participation; reLATTE may carry scoped receipts; staticID may authenticate operators; MEMENTO preserves evidence references. Do not claim integrations exist until tested.

## Fee model
Estimated net = gross minus fixed fees, percentage fees, FX spread, verification fees, network costs, payout and final transfer costs. Record who pays each charge. At illustrative 2.9% + $0.30 card pricing, 8,000 separate $1 transactions yield roughly $5,360 after per-charge rounding; 80 x $100 yield $7,744, before any collection or international costs. Compare ACH and sponsored processing using actual current provider terms. Avoid representing a provider-subsidized fee as a fee that does not exist.

## Build sequence and acceptance
- 002a: static fee calculator and route registry with dated sources, no real transfers, fixtures and deterministic tests for rounding, unavailable routes, missing costs and deadline constraints.
- 002b: test-only provider adapter interface, signed webhook fixtures, duplicate/out-of-order events, refunds, failure and reconciliation tests. No production credentials in frontend/repo.
- 002c: authorized station and sponsor workflows; consent, rate limits, caps, role checks, audit trail and privacy review.
- 002d: opt-in pilot with one verified receiver, provider approval, legal/compliance review, independent reconciliation and end-to-end test before enabling real payments. Kill switch and dated campaign closure; never reuse approval across cases automatically.

## Release criteria
No unverified recipient or account can be routed funds; no payment claims without evidence; no custody by Jubilee; no investment promises in gift flow; no hidden fees or unsupported zero-fee guarantees. Preserve a manual fallback and document net delivered, not just gross raised.
