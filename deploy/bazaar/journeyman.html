    What counts as success?

    One independently accepted external job, reviewed by a distinct person against the skill-specific check; control of the necessary assets and customer relationship; demonstrated repeatability without our intervention. Paid and unpaid outcomes stay separate. A later independent job is stronger evidence than a completed checklist. No ranking of people or compulsory mentorship.

    After departure

    Keep your files, methods and customers. Return only if useful. Teaching others is optional, never a condition of recognition. No automatic commission, exclusivity or fees for leaving.
