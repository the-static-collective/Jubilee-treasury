MOONCRATER 010 / JUBILEE CIRCUIT / FESTIVAL FOUNDRY

THE FEAST.

One table. One useful act. One more table.

Eat together. Discover what is possible. Leave something useful. Invite another independent host.

Proposed host kit, not a scheduled event or enrolled network. Food and help remain unconditional; worship, work, giving and future hosting are optional. No fundraising, commitments, attendance tracking or automatic submission.

THE GUILD · BAZAAR · Original Jubilee Circuit protocol

First Harbor / 20-minute table

After people have eaten and settled: 0–5 min, share cards voluntarily; 5–10 min, notice overlaps; 10–15 min, select at most one small act with a willing steward; 15–20 min, name the next action and optionally invite another host. This is a proposed adaptation, not a replacement for the original host kernel.

Full Feast / original two-hour rhythm

00–15 welcome and food · 15–35 music and optional response · 35–55 one authorized public need · 55–90 one bounded task · 90–110 verify what happened · 110–120 invite next voice.

Every receiving steward chooses or declines the act. Compare local purchase, knowledge transfer and donated resources. No one must work, sing, donate or return in order to eat.

Pass the table forward

Use opaque references and non-sensitive summaries only. This draft stays in the tab; downloaded JSON is unencrypted. Never enter private beneficiary details, addresses, credentials or names without permission.

Reproduction, not replication

The next host chooses the place, people, practices and purpose. Carry the lessons and invitations, not jurisdiction. A table without a successor is still worthwhile; a planned successor is not a completed gathering. Obtain host authority, accessibility, food safety and rights before an event.

Gather → collaborate → complete → witness → pass on → gather somewhere new. No real events, funds or completed acts are established by this page.
