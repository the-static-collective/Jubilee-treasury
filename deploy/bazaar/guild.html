The Guild charter

  • Every workshop owns its work, assets, customers and decisions.
  • Referrals require permission; do not share customer information without consent.
  • Resource sharing specifies custody, safety, condition and return.
  • Joint work requires a separate written agreement with actual participants before starting.
  • Gifts, paid work and shared equipment remain distinct; no implied dues or commission.
  • Leave freely; honor already accepted obligations or agree a release.

First field test: two genuinely independent makers choose one small cooperation, document terms privately, deliver a useful outcome and separately verify it. No participation or money is recognized by this desk.
