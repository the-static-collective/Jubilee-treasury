03 / REVIEW — HUMAN AUTHORITY

One case, one next useful action

  1. Identify the affected person's permission, need, deadline and actual decision-maker.
  2. Record a source reference for each material claim; preserve disagreement.
  3. Ask the question whose answer could rule out an entire proposed route.
  4. Record conditional offers with terms; do not count them as received funds.
  5. Explore alternatives: written extension, independent review, eligible existing resources or separately approved sponsorship.
  6. Hold real actions until the relevant people independently authorize them. Reconcile actual receipts separately.

Never infer consent, ownership, enforceability or funds from the research models. No automatic sending or publication.
