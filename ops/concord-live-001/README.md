## Operator
Read private views concord_paths and concord_next_questions. Column paths_unlocked_by_answer means routes affected by resolving a gate, not routes actually unlocked. Do not infer satisfaction from correspondence or a submission. A human reviewer records evidence reference and expiry for each gate; no sensitive evidence in public repo. Do not reuse anonymous signals as proof or permission. No currency conversion or combining unreviewed pledges. Church-held capacity derives only from recorded same-currency held stage and verified collecting stewards. Case status remains unverified until reviewed. Even a fully satisfied path is only REVIEW_READY.
