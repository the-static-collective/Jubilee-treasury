05 / What would verify its value?

Two people choosing, then fulfilling the exchange.

  1. Observe the object honestly. Say what is known and what is uncertain.
  2. Show it only to people who voluntarily expressed an interest.
  3. Find a specific proposed exchange: $8, a photo session, a song, or a gift.

  5. Only independently confirmed payment and delivery could establish a real completed exchange. This demo stops at HOLD.
