04 · The triple bottom line without coerced trade

People. Planet. Practical benefit.

Participants

Everyone retains the right to refuse. A deal must provide positive self-declared benefit to each affected participant.

Community

Sharing a machine or a room may make additional work and relationships possible.

Environment

Greater use of existing assets could avoid needless replacement, but the real environmental effect must be measured.

The platform proposes possible mutual gains. It does not select winners, set universal prices, or force anyone to exchange.
