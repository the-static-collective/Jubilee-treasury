## First three offers
- Flyer USD 25; one revision; digital PNG and print-ready PDF.
- Gathering kit USD 50; one poster, handout and screen announcement; one revision round.
- Landing page USD 150; one responsive informational page, up to five sections, source and one revision. Excludes domain/hosting/backend.
- No custom development before written scope; no speculative work to earn a commission. Use source-owned generic patterns only. Do not repackage client assets.

## Demand experiment (no spam)
Start with five personally relevant, consent-respecting conversations; one specific offer per prospect, not blanket solicitation. Prioritize existing warm leads: business owners with actual web/design needs, event organizers with upcoming events, churches seeking promotional materials. Do not assume the recipients of earlier nonprofit/volunteer correspondence consent to a commercial pitch. Do not put private names, emails or contact history into Git. Keep private prospect records outside this repository.

Suggested message: "I'm opening a small independent design and web workshop. We're testing three fixed-scope offers: $25 event flyers, $50 event design kits, and $150 informational landing pages. If you have an upcoming project that fits, I'd be glad to discuss the scope and timing. No obligation."

For an existing business collaborator, ask whether a small paid, clearly scoped deliverable would be useful this week, without assuming earlier work is paid or charging retrospectively.

## Measurement
For each private opportunity: local lead ref, source/consent, offer ID, proposed scope/price, contact date, reply, accepted scope, hours, direct costs, delivered date, independent acceptance, actual gross received, fees/refunds, net received, settlement receipt reference. Keep client data private. Pledge and draft = zero cash. Do not fabricate transactions. One accepted paid scope is a demand signal; one settled transaction and independent delivery acceptance is the first completed pilot. Compare time spent with direct paid work baseline.
If five suitable contacts produce zero positive signals, revise offer or audience rather than build more features. If price is rejected, record reason. If delivery capacity is inadequate, stop accepting inquiries.
