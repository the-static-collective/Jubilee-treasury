# MOONCRATER-011 — THE KEYHOLE

Find the smallest missing piece of information that materially changes which actions are possible.

## Four instruments

- ORACLE OF PRECEDENT: Find documented comparable outcomes. Record sources, time, eligibility, intermediary, evidence path, and what remains unknown. Similarity is not eligibility.
- LOCAL SHIELD: Find independent community contacts through public institutional directories; seek consent for any personal introduction. A directory entry is not a partnership.
- SWITCHBOARD: Inspect authorized incoming correspondence; categorize CAPITAL, WITNESS, BRIDGE, INTRODUCTION, KEYHOLE, TIME and DELIVERY FAILURE. Distinguish acknowledgment, conditional offer, actual acceptance, and verified outcome. No automatic forwarding or payment.
- KEYHOLE: List action prerequisites and evidence states. Rank missing facts first by actions whose only remaining evidence gate is that fact, then by other actions to which it contributes. Do not add confidence to claims just because they recur. A completed evidence gate is not execution authority.

## Private case template (keep outside public Git)

Case reference; decision deadline and source; exact question; fact ID; claim; evidence pointer; state (unknown/reported/verified/disputed); independent verification route; acquisition cost/time/safety; required actions; authorized decision owner; next reversible action. Keep actual documents, correspondence and personal identifiers in an authorized private location. Evidence pointers and labels are not authenticated by this code.

## Pressure test

Run node labs/mooncrater-001/keyhole.mjs from repository root. The fixture uses invented facts only. The ranking counts declared dependency relationships, not probabilities, monetary value or expected impact. Consider deadline, cost, privacy and risk separately. Do not claim one missing fact unlocks a jointly blocked action.

## Stop rules

Do not bypass local authority, claim verified need from a precedent, request funds into unverified accounts, expose a beneficiary, or turn an introduction into permission to act. A recipient may decline. Stop when a material contradiction appears and route to independent review.
