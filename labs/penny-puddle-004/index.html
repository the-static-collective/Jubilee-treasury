05 — More than a sale

Other useful doors

  • Keep it: Return it to its rightful owner.
  • Tell its story: An opt-in historical exhibit or photo narrative.
  • Find value: Pay a coin steward for genuinely useful examination, custody and preservation work from a separate sponsor budget.
  • Give value: With owner consent, direct actual net proceeds from an eventual sale toward a PUDDLE campaign.

Real collectible coins should not be cleaned or polished. Rare coins can be counterfeited; old pennies do not all weigh 2.50 grams. Do not use a color, scale reading or web price listing as proof of value.

PENNY face value ≠ potential collector premium ≠ settled cash. This experiment has no currency, legal escrow, buyer, custody, verified ownership or payouts. Everything runs locally and remains in memory.
